01 / WALLETS

A currency for each wallet

Create separate wallets for everyday life or travel. Choose currencies such as USD, EUR, GBP, IDR, SGD or MYR. Balances, debts and goals use the selected wallet currency.

Ruvy does not automatically convert currencies or combine balances in different currencies. The Telegram ledger currently uses IDR.

02 / RECORDS

Capture and review your spending

Record income and expenses using text or a form. Read receipt photos with OCR, then check the results before saving. Find records with search, categories and date filters.

Track debts, receivables, recurring bills and savings goals. View reports by wallet, and export or restore backups.

03 / RUVY AI

Ask Ruvy AI

Ask about spending, budgeting or savings goals during the free trial or on a paid plan. AI uses relevant summaries from your selected wallet; it is not individually trained on your records and does not remember previous conversations.

Responses can be incorrect, so check important figures. Daily limit: 5 questions during the free trial, 10 on paid plans. The AI service is being restored and may be temporarily unavailable.

04 / YOUR ACCOUNT

The same account across devices

Choose English or Bahasa Indonesia in the app. Sign in with the same account on your devices to sync your records; an internet connection is needed to update cloud records.

Your records remain yours. Export backups and use the account menu or support to request account and data deletion.

Understand your access before paying.

  • 2-day free trial: starts at registration, AI included (5 questions per day).
  • Monthly and yearly Google Play subscriptions: automatically renew until cancelled. Manage them through Google Play.

Google Play shows the applicable local price and billing period before purchase. Your wallet currency does not change your Google Play billing currency. Web purchases are one-time payments for the displayed access period.

When access expires, choose a plan to continue using the app. JSON backups remain downloadable from the plans screen. Read the terms of service for access, cancellation and refund details.
